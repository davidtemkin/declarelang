bidirectional bridges between Declare and the rest of the web — one hue, five controls, every surface follows.

I. HTML page hosting Declare apps

Each dark box is a <div data-declare-embed> with a full Declare app booted into it. The page holds each app's boot handle: it writes facts in as plain property assignments (dial.hue = v — constraints inside re-derive on their own) and watches facts out with observe(() => dial.hue, cb) — one callback per settle the value changed, nothing polling. The wiring between the two apps and this page is the imperative script at the bottom of this file; scenario II does the same job with constraints.

II. Declare app hosting web apps

One Declare app (crossings.declare) fills the box below, and the two inner panels are foreign: plain-JS apps this page mounts into the app's two DOMIsland views. What crosses is declared on each island as typed external facts — the master slider is pushed down to both tenants, tally's tap count flows up into the host's gauge and onward into orbit's dot count (a constraint chain crossing two app boundaries), orbit's speed flows up into the caption — and commands cross as post/onPost verbs (the reset button, tally's milestones, orbit's double-click burst). No wiring script: every arrow in that diagram is a one-line constraint.

crossings.declare hosts tally.js (plain DOM) and orbit.js (canvas, its own rAF clock, a native range input). Tap in tally → dots appear in orbit → the Declare gauge grows. Drag the master slider → both tenants recolor. Double-click the canvas → the host's log line reports the verb.

apps/two-way — scenario I wires by script, scenario II wires by constraint; the traffic is the same in both: facts one way or the other, verbs for the rest.
