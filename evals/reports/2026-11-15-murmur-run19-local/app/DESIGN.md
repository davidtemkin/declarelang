# Murmur — design statement

Murmur is meant to feel like paper and ink, not glass: a warm ground, one deep ink for your own
words, white cards for everyone else's, and in groups each person signed in their own colour.

The decision I'd defend: **history reads like a journal.** A new day is a serif heading set
between hairlines, not a grey pill. While you scroll back, the day you're in stays pinned at the
top in the same serif. Six years of Trail crew should read easily.

Each conversation is its own pane that is never torn down, so going back to one puts you
exactly where you left off.

Built by hand: the voice scrubber (the loudness bars are the track you press and drag) and the
icons. The library's `Slider` has no waveform. Everything else comes from the platform:
virtualisation, keeping your place, the socket, popovers, the viewer.
