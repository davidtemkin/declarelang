# Murmur

Paper and ink: a warm page, a serif for names and dates, and your own words in
one deep ink, so it reads as calm and personal, not another blue chat.

The decision I'd defend: **each person speaks in their own hue.** In a group,
their bubbles carry a pale wash of it, and their name and "writing…" line a
deep tone. You can tell who said what at a glance, before reading a single
name, and that is the hardest part of reading a busy group.

Infrastructure: the voice scrubber is my own `Control`. It draws the
recording's peaks as the track, and no library slider does that. The list's
glide is a ten-line `TweenLayout`, and the icons are drawn on the library's
`Icon`. Virtualization, keeping the reader's place, the socket, popovers and
the full-screen viewer all come from Declare's runtime and library.

Source: `declarelang/my-apps/murmur/`.
