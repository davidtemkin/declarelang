# Murmur

Paper, ink and one deep green: your words sit on the green, everyone else's on white. Persimmon means one thing only, something new: the badge, the NEW rule, the jump-down pill. In a group, each name appears once per run in that person's colour, and their face sits where the run ends.

**The decision I'd defend: time is told as distance, not as clocks.** Bubbles carry no timestamps. A new day gets a heading, and a silence of two hours or more is written as the silence ("3 hours later"). That's how a conversation actually moves. The exact time is a tooltip, and a date chip shows while you scroll back through six years.

**Infrastructure:** none of my own. Virtualization, keeping the reader's place, the socket and overlays all come from Declare. The waveform scrubber is drawn by hand because no library control fits it.
