# Murmur — design statement

Paper and ink. Your words are set in a deep ink bubble, everyone else's on
paper. One warm vermilion is kept for a single meaning: something here is new.

The decision I would defend is that **silence is spacing.** The gap above each
message grows with the logarithm of the pause before it: two points inside a
quick run, up to a cap after hours. A time is printed only where the
conversation actually paused or a new day began. So a burst reads as one
thought and a lull reads as a lull, without a timestamp on every line.

The voice scrubber is the recording's own waveform, and its width follows the
clip's length.

Infrastructure: none hand-built. Virtualized history, scroll anchoring and the
socket are Declare's own; the waveform and two small layouts are ordinary
Declare classes.
