# Murmur — design statement

I wanted it to feel like reading a letter, not like watching a feed. Warm
paper and dark ink carry the whole palette. Your words are set in ink and
everyone else's sit on stone. Ember is the only bright colour, and it means one
thing: something is new.

The decision I would defend is that time is told as distance. Nothing stamps a
clock on every line. Where a day begins it says so, and where
people went quiet it says "3 hours later · 14:02". Reading back, you see
the rhythm as well as the words.

The voice scrubber is the recording's own waveform: press anywhere on it to
jump there. A panorama too thin to read at full width is held at a legible
height and dragged sideways.

No infrastructure was built. The one piece of mechanism is reading-position
anchoring in `conversation.declare`.
