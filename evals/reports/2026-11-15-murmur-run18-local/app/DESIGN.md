# Murmur

Quiet paper, ink type, one strong colour: yours. Everyone else gets their own
hue, used for their initials, their name in a group and nothing else, so who
is speaking reads as colour before it is read as words.

**The decision I would defend: the reader's place belongs to the reader.**
Every conversation is its own live pane, built once and kept, so leaving and
returning finds you on the same words. Arrivals, reactions and late-loading
photographs never move the line you are reading; a pill says what came in
below. A "NEW" rule is fixed where your unread messages began and
stays put while you read.

Built here rather than taken from a library: the waveform scrubber (a
`ValueControl`, so keys and screen readers get a slider), the phone's sliding
stage, and the photo grid. The Declare library ships no waveform control or
slide-over stage.
