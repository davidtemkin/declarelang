# Murmur

Paper and ink: a warm page, your words set in a dark ink bubble, and a single
coral that only ever means *new*: the unread mark, the rule over the first
unread line, the note that something arrived below. A serif sets the
titles and the stamps over breaks.

The decision I'd defend: **in a group, every person gets a voice colour.** It
tints their bubbles, names and initials, so you can follow who is talking
before you read a word. Pairs stay plain white, because there it would be noise.

Nothing cuts. A conversation slides over the list, a photograph opens from where
it sits, reactions rise from their bubble, and a thread holds one anchored message
still while things grow around it.

No infrastructure was built. The one workaround: the App owns the socket, since
the runtime doesn't start a `Socket` declared inside a view-less model node.
