# Cadence — experience spec (written before the program)

**Ends**
1. Four seconds, from across the room, answers *where am I*: this week's volume, the
   streak, the week's shape, the latest thing that happened.
2. A live session is unmistakably *running* — it moves while you watch it.
3. Fourteen months are one surface you **handle**: push it through the months, pull it
   open to a fortnight, squeeze it to a season. It never waits for you to let go.
4. Picking a session says what it was and *where it sits against everything else*.
5. Recording a session is confirming, not filling in — a few taps, no keyboard.
6. Everything derived from the history is true of a new session the instant it lands.

**Tokens (literal, 1:1)**
`4 sessions · 3h 40m` · `12 days` / `no streak` · `3h 40m` / `45m` · `12.4 km` · `148 bpm`
Largest number ≥ 6× smallest text on the same screen. At most two families.

**Constraints (absolute)**
No accounts, no login, no settings screen. No server of my own — 8320 is the whole
backend. The service's frozen clock (`/api/today`) is the one to trust. Phone width and
desktop width, one build.

**Means demoted to evidence**
"dashboard" → not a card grid; an editorial stack with two rules and no boxes.
"chart" → not a picture; a surface with a camera (two scalars) that everything derives from.
"modal / form" → a sprung sheet on the phone, a sprung panel at the desk; one tree.
"refresh" → nothing refreshes; a write to the store wakes every derivation.

**Absences the brief could not ask for** (what travels)
- The week total, the streak, and the period summary **move** to new values, never cut.
- The week's bars grow from the ground when they first arrive.
- A newly added session that is off-screen brings the year surface to it.
- The live session breathes: elapsed seconds advance continuously, heart rate refreshes.

**Resolved conflicts / judgment calls (reported)**
- "It reads exactly: **4 sessions · 3h 40m**" — rendered as one line, exactly those words
  in that order, but typeset in two sizes: the numerals huge, the words small. A single
  uniform run cannot be both the exact sentence and six times the smallest text at a
  phone's width. The words are as written; only their size varies.
- Effort, not sport, earns the one accent. Sports are distinguished typographically
  (`RUN` `RIDE` `LIFT` `SWIM`), never by a second colour.
- Streak = consecutive days ending today, or ending yesterday when today is still
  young (a live session does not count until it is recorded).
- Week = Monday-first.
