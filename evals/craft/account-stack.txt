A request that's separate from the build — please don't change anything. Now that you've built {App} in {Platform}, write a candid account of how the build went. Please cover:

(a) Choices — the libraries and structure you chose, and why; what you looked up during the build, and why.

(b) The first time it ran — what was wrong when you first saw it running, and how you found each problem.

(c) Every error and surprise — each build or type error, each runtime error, and each result that wasn't what you wrote it to do. For each: what you wrote, what happened, what you changed, and whether the cause was your code, the brief, a library, or the tooling. Say "unsure" where you are.

(d) Verifying it — how you checked your work, what each check caught, and what you left unchecked.

(e) What was hard — the parts of the brief that took the most effort, and why. For each, say whether it was one obstacle or a pattern, roughly what it cost you, and what you would need to have written it right the first time.

(f) Confidence — how sure you are that the app does what the brief asks, part by part, and where you would expect a tester to find problems.

(g) Gaps you worked around — each place where you wanted something the platform didn't seem to offer, so you wrote it another way. For each: what you wanted, where you looked and what you tried, what you did instead, and your judgement now: does the platform really lack it, or might it be there and you didn't find it or see how?

(h) Changes of direction — anything you built one way and then replaced with a different approach before you finished. For each: what it was first, what it became, roughly when, and what made you change: a refusal, a finding, something you saw running, the brief, or your own second thoughts.

Be specific — name files, libraries, errors and moments from this build — and say plainly where your judgement is uncertain.
